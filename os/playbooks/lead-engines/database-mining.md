# Lead Engine: Database Mining

**Job:** Manufacture warm conversations TODAY from people already in the
dealership's orbit. This is the fastest path from "no one to talk to" to a full
day of real conversations.

**Scripts live in:** `../../scripts/email/database-mining.md`,
`../../scripts/phone/phone-scripts.md`, `../../scripts/text/text-scripts.md`
**Daily dose:** `/daily` assigns one play + a 10-outreach target. Ten a day =
200+/month = a second pipeline.

All lists come out of DriveCentric (and the DMS/service scheduler where you
have access). Ask your manager which mined lists are open season vs. assigned —
orphan owners especially are usually first-come, first-served.

---

## Play 1: Orphan owners (highest priority — they're already yours to take)

**Who:** Customers whose original salesperson left the store. They bought from
Berger, get serviced at Berger, and currently have *no* salesperson.

**The play:**
1. Pull/request the orphan list from DriveCentric (filter: sold customers with
   inactive/terminated salesperson).
2. Work it oldest-purchase-first — 3+ year owners are in the sweet spot for
   trade equity and lease-end.
3. First touch = **introduction, not pitch**: "I've been asked to take care of
   you going forward" (script: `email/database-mining.md` §Orphan intro).
4. Offer something concrete and free: equity check, service coordination,
   recall check.
5. Log every one you claim; they enter your lifetime cadence
   (see `referrals-repeat.md`).

**Cadence per orphan:** intro (day 0) → value follow-up (day 4) → call (day 7)
→ then quarterly lifetime cadence.

## Play 2: Lease renewals (appointments on rails)

**Who:** Leases maturing in the next **3–6 months** (start at 6; urgency rises
as maturity approaches).

**Why it works:** They MUST do something — turn in, buy out, or re-lease. You're
not creating a need, just getting there first with a plan.

**The play:**
1. Monthly: pull leases maturing in 90–180 days.
2. Touch sequence: "your lease-end options" email (day 0) → call (day 3) →
   text with a specific upgrade option (day 10).
3. Frame as **options review**, never "come buy a car": turn-in process, buyout
   number, and what a new lease looks like — often at a similar payment.
4. Book a 30-minute lease-end review appointment. That's the close of the call.

## Play 3: Equity mining (the "you're in a better position than you think" call)

**Who:** Owners 24–48 months into a purchase whose trade value puts them near or
above payoff — strong used-car values make this list bigger than people think.

**The play:**
1. Work a model/year band at a time (e.g., 2022–2023 Equinox and Silverado
   buyers) so your value claims are grounded in real current numbers from the
   desk/appraisal tool.
2. Message: "Vehicles like yours are worth more than you'd expect right now —
   want me to run your exact number? Takes me 10 minutes, costs you nothing."
3. Deliver the number **with** a same-payment-range upgrade option.
4. Honest framing only — never promise equity you haven't verified.

**Bonus variant:** the used-car buyback angle — "we need clean used trucks like
yours; I can get you a real offer this week whether or not you buy anything."

## Play 4: Service-drive conversions (buyers standing in the building)

**Who:** Service customers, especially: big repair estimates on aging vehicles,
customer-pay repairs > $1,500, 5+ year-old vehicles in for anything major.

**The play:**
1. Befriend the service advisors (see `referrals-repeat.md` §Internal network —
   they are your best bird dogs; take them coffee).
2. Ask them to flag: declined big repairs, "is it even worth fixing?" comments.
3. Approach softly IN PERSON while they wait, or by call after the visit:
   "Before you put $2,800 into it, want to see what it's worth as a trade and
   what that money does as a down payment instead?" (script:
   `phone/phone-scripts.md` §Service drive).
4. Never ambush at the cashier. Offer math, not pressure.

## Play 5: Unsold / be-back re-engagement (money left on the floor)

**Who:** Everyone who visited, quoted, or applied in the last 90–180 days and
didn't buy. Store-wide, ~80% of ups don't buy day one — and most never get a
quality follow-up after week two. That's your inventory.

**The play:**
1. Weekly: pull your (and, if allowed, house) unsold from DriveCentric,
   30–180 days old, dead or dormant status.
2. Re-open with **new information**, never "just checking in": the exact unit
   they wanted just got a price drop / a similar one landed / incentives changed
   this month / their trade is worth more now.
3. Sequence: new-info text or email (day 0) → call (day 2) → video of a
   matching unit (day 7).
4. If truly dead: one graceful "am I okay to close your file?" message — the
   takeaway close revives a surprising number.
