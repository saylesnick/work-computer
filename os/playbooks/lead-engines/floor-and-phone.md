# Lead Engine: Floor & Phone

**Job:** Squeeze maximum yield from what the store already hands you — internet
leads, phone-ups, and walk-ins. This isn't lead *generation*, it's lead
*conservation*: most salespeople leak 60–70% of these. You won't.

**Scripts live in:** `../../scripts/email/internet-lead-cadence.md`,
`../../scripts/phone/phone-scripts.md`
**Skills:** `/new-lead` builds the first-touch pack; `/follow-up` builds every
touch after.

---

## 1. Internet leads: speed × quality × persistence

Three numbers decide internet-lead income:

**Speed.** Contact within 5 minutes vs. 30 minutes changes contact rates by
several ×. The lead is comparison-shopping 3–5 stores in one sitting; first
quality response usually gets the appointment. When a lead drops: call first
(twice, back to back — second call catches them), then text, then email, all
within 10 minutes. `/new-lead` produces all three pieces in seconds.

**Quality of first touch.** The store's auto-responder already sent generic
fluff. You win by being specific:
- Reference the exact vehicle AND answer the question they asked.
- Add one piece of NEW information (real availability, a just-shot video of the
  actual unit, a sister unit worth seeing).
- One clear, easy next step: two appointment options, not "let me know."
- A 45–60s personal video of the actual vehicle is the single biggest
  differentiator available. "Shot this for you 10 minutes ago" is unanswerable
  by other stores' templates.

**Persistence.** Most internet leads buy within 30–90 days; most salespeople
quit after 2–3 touches in week one. The 8-touch cadence
(`email/internet-lead-cadence.md`): day 0 ×2 (call+text), day 1, day 3, day 5,
day 8, day 12, day 18, day 30 — each touch adds new information, never "just
following up." Then monthly until they buy or say stop. DriveCentric schedules
it; the scripts make each touch worth reading.

## 2. Phone-ups: sell the appointment, not the car

One goal on every inbound call: **a firm appointment**, defined as a specific
time + both parties confirming. Not "swing by whenever."

The call frame (full scripts: `phone/phone-scripts.md` §Phone-up):
1. **Enthusiasm + name + trade for theirs.** Get their name and number in the
   first minute ("in case we get cut off").
2. **Answer, then advance.** Answer their question honestly, then attach the
   next question: availability → "It's here — when are you free to see it, early
   week or weekend?"
3. **Never desk a deal on the phone.** Price shoppers: give honest ranges and
   the reason numbers need the vehicle + trade in front of us; offer to
   have real numbers ready AT the appointment.
4. **Alternative-choice appointment close:** "Today at 4:30 or tomorrow at 10?"
   Then anchor it: "I'll have it pulled up front with a sold-pending tag on it."
5. **Confirm like pre-sale says** (`../sale-process/pre-sale.md`): recap text
   immediately with your name/photo, address, and the vehicle.

## 3. Walk-ins: convert lookers into booked buyers

The up you don't sell today is still a lead — IF you get contact info and a
reason to talk again.

- **Meet & greet** sets the whole tone — see `../sale-process/during-sale.md`.
- **The 100% rule: no up leaves without** (a) their contact info, (b) your
  card + a reason they'll hear from you ("I'll send you the CarFax and that
  payment breakdown tonight"), and (c) an attempted next appointment.
- **"Just looking"** = "I don't trust you yet." Response: lower pressure,
  raise usefulness. "Perfect — looking is free. What are we ruling out today,
  trucks or SUVs?" Walk with them, be a guide, earn the sit-down.
- **The turn:** if they're leaving without buying, offer a manager TO (store
  process), then lock the be-back: book the return appointment before they
  leave, text them the vehicle photos before they're out of the lot ("so you
  have my number").
- Every unsold up gets logged into DriveCentric same-shift with a real note
  (vehicle, motivation, objection, next step) — that note is what makes the
  be-back sequence in `database-mining.md` §Play 5 work.

## 4. BDC & round-robin relationships

If Berger's BDC sets appointments: BDC agents decide whose appointments get the
extra polish. Same play as service advisors — gratitude, communication, coffee.
Confirm their appointments personally so their show rate (their scorecard)
goes up when the customer is yours.

## Metrics (via `/log` → `/review`)

- Internet leads: contact rate, appointment rate, avg touches before response
- Phone-ups: appointment-set rate (target 50%+), show rate (target 70% with
  confirmation system)
- Walk-ins: info-capture rate (target 100%), same-day close rate, be-back rate
- Deals with Source Engine = Internet / Phone / Walk-in
