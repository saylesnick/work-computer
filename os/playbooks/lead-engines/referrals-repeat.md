# Lead Engine: Referrals & Repeat

**Job:** Turn every sold customer into 2–3 future deals (their next purchase +
their referrals), and build a network of people who send you buyers. Referral
leads close at 2–3× the rate of any other source and negotiate the least.
This engine is how veterans sell 20+ units without taking ups.

**Scripts live in:** `../../scripts/email/sold-followup.md`,
`../../scripts/text/text-scripts.md`
**Tracking:** Airtable → Referral Network table + Deals (Source Engine =
"Referral" / "Repeat").

---

## 1. The referral engine (asks that actually work)

The #1 reason salespeople don't get referrals: they don't ask, or they ask once
at delivery when the customer is exhausted. The system asks **small, specific,
and repeatedly at high-satisfaction moments**:

**The moments to ask:**
1. **Delivery (soft plant, not the ask):** "My goal is that when anyone you know
   needs a vehicle, I'm the first person you think of. I take care of the people
   my customers send me."
2. **3-day check-in (the real first ask):** They're at peak happiness. "Quick
   favor — who's the next person in your world who'll need a vehicle? A name is
   all I need; I'll take great care of them."
3. **Every lifetime-cadence touch thereafter:** one light line, not a speech.
4. **The moment they compliment you** — anywhere, any time: "That means a lot.
   Best compliment you could give me is sending me someone you care about."

**Make it concrete, not abstract:** "who do you know that's looking" gets
nothing. "Anyone in your family with a lease ending this year?" / "Any of your
coworkers commuting in something they complain about?" gets names.

**Incentive framing:** If Berger has a bird-dog/referral bonus, lead with what
the REFERRED person gets (VIP treatment), mention the reward second, and always
follow store/legal rules on referral payments. If no program: your currency is
guaranteed white-glove treatment + a thank-you (handwritten note, small gift
card where policy allows).

**When a referral closes:** the referrer gets a call from you personally within
24 hours + the thank-you. Log it in Referral Network. Nothing kills a referral
tree faster than an unthanked referrer.

## 2. Sold-customer lifetime cadence (the repeat machine)

Average trade cycle is ~3–4 years. Whoever is present in year 2 and 3 gets the
next deal — usually not the salesperson who sold it, because they vanished.
Every sold customer enters this cadence (log in DriveCentric as tasks; scripts
in `email/sold-followup.md`):

| Touch | When | What |
|---|---|---|
| Delivery+1 day | Text | "How was night one?" + you're their contact for anything |
| Day 3 | Call | Everything working? Bluetooth/OnStar/features set up? → first referral ask |
| Week 2 | Email/text | CSI heads-up handled earlier; now: review ask if not done, service intro |
| Day 45 | Text | First-service reminder offer ("want me to set it up?") |
| Every 90 days | Any | Value touch: recall check, feature tip, market-value update, seasonal (winter prep) |
| Birthday | Text/card | Personal, zero pitch |
| Purchase anniversary | Text | "One year today — quick equity check on the house?" |
| Month 30–36 (or lease month −6) | Call | The next-vehicle conversation (equity/renewal play from `database-mining.md`) |

Rule: **at least 6 touches/year per sold customer, at most 2 of them salesy.**

## 3. Local network building (bird dogs)

Build a roster of 20–30 people who talk to lots of people: barbers, gym staff,
realtors, insurance agents, small-business owners, bartenders, youth-sports
coaches, your own service advisors.

**The play:**
- Be their customer first. Be memorable and useful.
- The line: "I take care of anyone you send me like family — and I'll send
  people your way too." Reciprocity is the whole strategy.
- Track them in Airtable → Referral Network: who, where, last touch, next touch,
  deals sent. `/daily` surfaces the ones going cold (no touch in 30+ days).
- Touch monthly-ish: a coffee, a share of their business's post, a genuine
  check-in. NOT a pitch.

**Internal network is part of this:** service advisors, the BDC, F&I, techs.
They meet future buyers all day. Coffee and gratitude are the best marketing
spend in the building.

## 4. Reviews as a referral multiplier

Every 5-star Google review naming you is a referral to strangers. The ask lives
in the post-sale playbook (`../sale-process/post-sale.md`), but count it here:
reviews naming Nick are a Referral-engine metric. Target: **every delivery gets
one review ask; 1 in 3 lands.**

## Metrics (via `/log` → `/review`)

- Referral asks made / week (Activity Log — target: 1 per customer touch)
- Names received / appointments from referrals
- Referral Network touches/week; contacts gone cold (>30 days)
- Deals with Source Engine = Referral or Repeat — the long-run goal is this
  line becoming your #1 source.
