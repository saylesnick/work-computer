# Lead Engine: Social & Video

**Job:** Make Nick the known car person in Grand Rapids, so strangers arrive
already trusting him. This engine compounds — it's slow for 60–90 days, then it
never stops producing.

**Scripts live in:** `../../scripts/social/` and `../../scripts/text/`
**Skill:** `/social` batches a week of this in one sitting.

---

## 1. The identity

You are not "Berger Chevrolet's page." You are **Nick, the guy in Grand Rapids
who makes car buying easy**. Personal profile + professional consistency.
Everything posts under your name; the store gets mentioned, not worshipped.

Platform priorities, in order:
1. **Facebook** (personal profile + Marketplace) — where West Michigan car
   buyers actually are.
2. **Instagram** — reels mirror the FB videos; stories for behind-the-scenes.
3. **TikTok** (optional, once the habit is solid) — same vertical videos.

Check Berger's social-media policy once before ramping up — most stores love
this; a few want review of pricing claims. Stay inside whatever they allow.

## 2. Facebook Marketplace listing system (fastest inbound)

Marketplace is the highest-intent free lead source in retail automotive.

**The system:**
- List **3–5 used units at a time** under your own profile (confirm store policy
  on personal listings; many stores encourage it).
- Pick units with strong photo appeal and clear value stories — not just aged
  inventory the desk wants gone.
- Refresh/renew listings **every 7 days**; delete-and-relist stale ones.
- Photos: 10+ — front 3/4, rear 3/4, interior driver's view, dash/odometer,
  back seat, cargo, wheels, any flaws (honesty converts), one of you with the
  vehicle.
- Description template: `scripts/social/marketplace-listing.md` — hook line,
  5 value bullets, honest condition note, clear CTA to message you.

**Reply speed is the whole game:** Marketplace shoppers message five listings
at once. First helpful human reply usually wins the appointment. Target
**under 5 minutes** during shift; reply templates in
`scripts/social/marketplace-replies.md` get the conversation to phone/text,
then to an appointment — never negotiate price in Messenger.

## 3. The content cadence (the trust machine)

**Minimum viable cadence: 1 post per day, 3+ videos per week.** Batched weekly
via `/social`, posted daily.

Weekly content mix (the 5-3-2 rule, adapted):
- **3× value/inventory** — walkaround video, "just landed" unit, deal of the
  week, "what $350/mo gets you right now"-style posts (numbers only from real
  desk quotes).
- **2× education** — answer real buyer questions: lease vs. buy, what your trade
  is really worth, how to shop rates, EV tax credit basics, winter tires in GR.
  These posts get shared and searched.
- **1× social proof** — delivery photo/video with the customer (with their
  permission), review screenshot, milestone ("family #14 this month").
- **1× human** — you, the team, the store dog, Grand Rapids life. People buy
  from people.

## 4. The video system (highest-leverage skill in the store)

Three video types, all shot on a phone, vertical, 30–90 seconds, natural light,
hook in the first 3 seconds. Shot lists in `scripts/social/video-shot-lists.md`.

1. **Inventory walkaround** — one unit, three best features, one honest
   drawback, CTA. Also send these **1:1** to specific customers ("shot this for
   you before it hits the site") — personal video texts routinely double
   response rates vs. plain text.
2. **Delivery video** — 15 seconds, new owner with their vehicle, one line from
   them. Ask permission, tag them if they're comfortable. This is referral fuel
   AND content.
3. **Answer-a-question** — take the question a customer asked you this week,
   answer it to camera. Infinite topic supply.

## 5. Local groups strategy

Join 5–10 Grand Rapids / West Michigan buy-sell-trade, neighborhood, and
interest groups. **Rules:**
- Be a helpful human 90% of the time (answer car questions, no pitch).
- Never spam listings into groups that prohibit it.
- When someone posts "looking for a reliable SUV under $20k" — answer publicly
  with genuine help, then offer to DM options. That's a lay-down lead.

## 6. Metrics (log via `/log`, review via `/review`)

- Posts published / videos shot per week (Activity Log)
- Marketplace listings live + refreshed (Activity Log)
- Inbound conversations started from social/Marketplace (Activity Log)
- Sold deals with Source Engine = "Social/Marketplace" (Deals)

**Health check:** after 90 days of real cadence, this engine should produce
conversations weekly. If not, `/review` inspects: cadence actually kept? reply
speed? content mix too salesy?
