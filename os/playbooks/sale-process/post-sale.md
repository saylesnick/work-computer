# Sale Process: Post-Sale

**Job:** The sale ends; the asset begins. Delivery and the first 30 days decide
three numbers that pay you for years: CSI score, review count, and referral
flow. This playbook hands the customer into the lifetime cadence
(`../lead-engines/referrals-repeat.md` §2) with all three secured.

---

## 1. The delivery experience (make it an event)

People remember peaks and endings. Delivery is both.

- Vehicle detailed, full-enough tank per store policy, staged separately from
  inventory — bow optional but the *separate, waiting, ready* feeling is not.
- **Tech setup is the modern delivery:** phone paired, favorites set, Chevy app
  installed and connected, OnStar/Wi-Fi trial activated, driver profile,
  key features demoed (trailering app, Super Cruise, EV charging setup —
  whatever applies). A customer who leaves confused becomes a bad survey; a
  customer who leaves set up becomes a promoter.
- Introduce service lane + how to schedule; set the first-service expectation.
- **The photo/video moment:** "Mind if we grab a picture with it? I love
  celebrating this." → delivery content (with permission) for the social
  engine + a copy texted to them (they'll share it — that's *their* network
  seeing *your* work).
- **Plant the referral seed** (soft, from `referrals-repeat.md` §1): "When
  anyone you know needs a vehicle, I want to be the first call. I take care of
  the people my customers send."

## 2. CSI protection (the survey talk, done right)

- Set the expectation transparently: "You'll get a survey from GM/the store.
  It's pass/fail for me — anything less than top marks counts as failing. If
  ANYTHING about today wasn't a 10, tell me right now and I'll fix it before
  you leave."
- This is the ethical version: surface and fix real problems before they become
  survey problems. Never coach dishonesty, never beg.
- Any wobble (a scratch found at delivery, a we-owe item) → written we-owe,
  owned loudly, followed up personally until closed. Problems handled well
  score HIGHER than no problems.

## 3. The review ask (Google, naming you)

Timing: **day 2–3, at the happiness peak**, right after the check-in call
confirms all is well — not in the delivery paperwork blizzard.

The ask (scripts: `../../scripts/text/text-scripts.md` §Review ask):
"It was genuinely great working with you. Two-minute favor that helps me more
than anything else: a Google review — and if you mention me by name, that's
how the next family finds me." + the direct review link.

- One ask + one gentle nudge a week later if not done. Never more.
- A review comes in → thank them personally same day, screenshot to the
  Content Calendar (social proof post), log it in `/log`.
- Unhappy signal instead? That's a gift: fix it first, never ask an unhappy
  customer for a review.

## 4. The first-30-days sequence (where CSI, reviews, and referrals get won)

| Touch | When | Job |
|---|---|---|
| Night-of text | Delivery evening | "How's the first drive feel?" — catches issues instantly |
| Day 3 call | Day 3 | Feature questions answered, everything-perfect check → **review ask + first referral ask** |
| Week 2 | Email | Owner resources: app tips, service scheduling, your direct line — the "I'm not going anywhere" message |
| Day 30 | Text | One-month check + "any lights, noises, questions?" |
| Day 45 | Text | First-service reminder ("want me to book it?") — ties them to Berger service, which ties them to you |

Then the customer flows into the quarterly lifetime cadence in
`referrals-repeat.md`. Log all of it as DriveCentric tasks at delivery — the
whole sequence gets scheduled in 5 minutes while the deal is fresh.

## 5. Close the loop in the OS

Same day as delivery:
- `/log` the deal: unit, front/back gross if known, **Source Engine** (which
  playbook produced this customer — the OS's most important data point).
- Referral Network: add anyone new the deal surfaced (the referrer, the
  co-signer who liked you, the customer's business).
- Content Calendar: delivery photo/video queued (if permitted).

## Metrics

- Review asks made ÷ deliveries (target: 100%) and reviews landed (target: 1 in 3)
- Referral asks at day-3 call (target: 100%)
- First-30-days touches completed on time (binary per deal)
- CSI: perfect surveys ÷ returned
