# Text Scripts (all scenarios)

**Compliance rule (non-negotiable):** texts go only to customers who opted in
through dealership tools, and only via DriveCentric / the dealership texting
platform — never personal SMS blasts. Include opt-out respect: any "stop" is
honored instantly and logged.

Texts are 1–3 sentences. One idea, one next step. First-ever text always
includes your name + store.

---

## Internet lead / new lead

- **Day 0 (right after the 2 calls):** "Hi {{name}}, it's Nick at Berger
  Chevrolet — you asked about the {{year}} {{model}}. It's here on the lot.
  Want me to send a quick video of the actual one?"
- **Video follow:** "Here's your {{model}}: {{link}}. The {{feature}} is even
  better in person. Today at 4:30 or tomorrow morning to see it?"

## Appointment confirmations (pre-sale system)

- **Set (immediately):** "Locked in: {{day}} at {{time}}, Berger Chevrolet,
  {{address}}. I'm putting a sold-pending tag on the {{model}} so it's here
  for you. — Nick {{photo first time}}"
- **Night before:** "{{name}}, your {{model}} just got washed and I pulled the
  window sticker for you. All set for {{time}} tomorrow?"
- **2 hours out:** "See you at {{time}} — park in the guest spots by the main
  door and I'll meet you outside. — Nick"
- **No-show, same day:** "Missed you today — zero stress. It's still tagged
  for you through tomorrow. Tonight at 6 or Saturday morning better?"

## Be-back / unsold

- "{{name}}, Nick at Berger. The {{model}} you drove {{when}} just
  {{new info: dropped $X / got a twin in {{color}}}}. Want fresh photos?"
- **Left without buying (before they're off the lot):** "Great meeting you
  today. Here are the photos of the {{model}} + my card so you have my number.
  I'll hold the figures we discussed through {{day}}."
- **Graceful close:** "No pressure ever — should I close your file, or is the
  search just paused? Either answer is fine."

## Sold customer — first 30 days

- **Delivery night:** "First drive home in the {{model}} — how'd it feel?
  Anything at all, I'm your first call. — Nick"
- **Day 30:** "One month in the {{model}}. Any lights, noises, or
  questions? And how's the {{feature they cared about}} treating you?"
- **Day 45 service:** "First service is due around {{date/mileage}}. Want me
  to book it for you? Takes me 2 minutes."

## Review ask (day 2–3, after the happy check-in call)

- "That genuinely made my day. Two-minute favor that helps me more than
  anything: a Google review — mention me by name and that's how the next
  family finds me. {{review link}}"
- **Gentle nudge (1 week later, once, only if warm):** "No worries if life got
  busy — here's that review link again in case you have 2 minutes: {{link}}.
  Either way, enjoy the {{model}}."

## Referral asks (light-touch lines to append)

- "Quick one: anyone in your family with a lease ending this year? I'll take
  great care of them."
- "Any coworkers complaining about their commute car? Send them my way —
  VIP treatment, promise."
- **When compliments arrive:** "Best compliment you could give me is sending
  me someone you care about."

## Lifetime cadence

- **Birthday:** "Happy birthday, {{name}}! Hope the {{model}} gets you
  somewhere fun today. — Nick at Berger"
- **Anniversary:** "One year with the {{model}} today. Curious what it's
  worth after a year? I'll run your number free — people are usually
  surprised."
- **Equity ping:** "Vehicles like your {{model}} are bringing strong money
  right now. Want your exact number? 10 minutes, no strings."

## Referral network (bird dogs)

- "{{name}} — sent {{referred person}} your way for {{their business}} this
  week. Hope they treat you as well as you treat my people."
- "Coffee's on me this week — when works? (Also: anyone you know car
  shopping? You know the drill.)"
