# Email Scripts: Internet Lead 8-Touch Cadence

Voice rules: short, specific, one clear next step, no fake urgency, max one
exclamation point. Every touch carries NEW information — never "just following
up." Send from work email / DriveCentric. Pair each with the matching call/text
from the cadence in `../../playbooks/lead-engines/floor-and-phone.md` §1.

---

## Touch 1 — Day 0 (within 10 minutes, after the 2 calls + text)

**Subject:** Your {{year}} {{model}} — it's here, quick answer inside

Hi {{name}},

Thanks for reaching out about the {{year}} {{model}} ({{color}}, stock
#{{stock}}). Quick answers:

- **{{their question, e.g., "Is it available?"}}** — {{honest answer}}.
- It's physically on our lot; I just walked past it.
- {{one NEW piece of info: incentive, recent price drop, notable option}}

I can have it pulled up front whenever works. Are you more of an
early-in-the-week person or a weekend person?

{{signature}}

> If possible, attach/link a 45–60s walkaround video of the ACTUAL unit:
> "Shot this for you just now so you can see the real one."

## Touch 2 — Day 1

**Subject:** Video of your {{model}} (the actual one)

{{name}} — shot a quick walkaround of the exact {{model}} you asked about so
you're not guessing from stock photos: {{link}}.

The {{one honest standout feature}} is even better in person. Want me to hold
it for a 20-minute look {{day option A}} or {{day option B}}?

## Touch 3 — Day 3

**Subject:** Two more worth seeing next to it

Most folks comparing the {{model}} also look at {{sister unit 1}} and
{{sister unit 2}} — we have both. Seeing them side by side usually settles it
in about 15 minutes. Worth a quick visit this week?

## Touch 4 — Day 5

**Subject:** What your {{trade vehicle / "current vehicle"}} is worth right now

Used values move monthly, and vehicles like yours are strong right now. I can
run your exact number in 10 minutes with no obligation — a lot of my customers
are surprised how much it changes the math. Want me to run it?

## Touch 5 — Day 8

**Subject:** This month's {{model}} numbers

{{name}} — current incentives on the {{model}}: {{real incentive/rate from the
desk}}. These reset at month-end, so if the math matters to your timing, this
is the info to have. Happy to put together an exact out-the-door for you —
takes me 15 minutes. Want it?

## Touch 6 — Day 12

**Subject:** Still the right fit? Honest question

Quick honesty check: is the {{model}} still on your list, or has your search
moved? Either answer helps me help you — if it's moved, tell me where and I'll
point you at the right units instead (even if it's not what you first asked
about).

## Touch 7 — Day 18

**Subject:** {{new inventory/new info hook}}

{{2–3 sentences on genuinely new info: a fresh trade-in like their target, a
price drop, a color they wanted arriving}}. Thought of you first. Want photos
before it hits the website?

## Touch 8 — Day 30 (the graceful takeaway)

**Subject:** Should I close your file?

{{name}} — I don't want to be inbox clutter. If the timing's changed or you
bought elsewhere, no hard feelings at all — just tell me and I'll close your
file. If you're still looking, tell me that too and I'll keep doing the legwork.
Which is it?

> After Touch 8: monthly value-touch only (new inventory matching them, market
> updates) until they buy or opt out. They flow into the be-back play:
> `../../playbooks/lead-engines/database-mining.md` §Play 5.
