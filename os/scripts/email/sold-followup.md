# Email Scripts: Sold-Customer Lifetime Cadence

Companions to `../../playbooks/lead-engines/referrals-repeat.md` §2 and
`../../playbooks/sale-process/post-sale.md`. Most early touches are texts/calls
(see `../text/text-scripts.md`); these are the email touches.

## Week 2 — Owner resources

**Subject:** Everything you need for the {{model}} (save this one)

Hi {{name}},

Two weeks in — hope the {{model}} already feels like yours. A few things worth
saving:

- **Chevy app:** remote start, tire pressure, service scheduling from your
  phone. If we didn't finish setting it up at delivery, I'll do it with you in
  5 minutes next time you're by.
- **Service:** book online at {{service link}} or just text me and I'll set it
  up. First oil change is due around {{mileage/date}}.
- **Me:** {{phone}}. Any light, noise, or question — I'm your first call, and
  I'll chase the answer so you don't have to.

Enjoy it. That color was the right call.

Nick

## Day 90 / quarterly value touch (rotate these)

**Subject options + one-liner bodies:**

- **"Recall & software check on your {{model}}: all clear"** — Ran your VIN
  today as part of my quarterly check on my customers — {{result}}. Nothing
  you need to do; just like keeping you ahead of it.
- **"Your {{model}} is holding its value well"** — Quick market note:
  {{model}}s like yours are trading strong. If you ever want your exact
  number, it takes me 10 minutes.
- **"One feature most {{model}} owners never find"** — {{feature tip relevant
  to their vehicle}}. Try it this week and tell me it isn't great.
- **"Michigan winter prep"** (Oct/Nov) — Tires, battery, washer fluid that
  doesn't freeze: the 3 things worth doing before the first real snow. Want me
  to set up a winter-check appointment in service?

*(Every quarterly touch may carry ONE light referral line, e.g., "If anyone in
your world starts car shopping, send them my way — I'll treat them like I
treated you." Max 2 salesy touches per year total.)*

## Purchase anniversary

**Subject:** One year with the {{model}} today

{{name}} — a year ago today you drove it home. Two quick things: happy
anniversary, and if you're curious what it's worth after a year (people
usually are), I'll run your number free anytime. Either way — glad you're in
my corner of the world.

Nick

## Month 30–36 — the next-vehicle conversation

**Subject:** The math on your {{model}} just got interesting

{{name}} — you're at the point in ownership where the math sometimes flips:
between what {{model}}s are bringing and current programs, some of my customers
can move into a new one at {{honest framing: "a similar payment" / "less than
they expect"}} — and some are better off keeping what they have.

I can tell you which camp you're in with 15 minutes and your odometer reading.
Want me to run it?

Nick
