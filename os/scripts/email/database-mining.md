Nick

## Lease renewal — first touch (Play 2)

**Subject:** Your {{model}} lease: 3 options, zero pressure

Hi {{name}},

Your lease on the {{year}} {{model}} matures around {{maturity month}}. Most
people don't hear from anyone until the last minute, and then they're rushed.
Let's not do that.

You've got three options, and I'll lay all of them out honestly:

1. **Turn it in** — I'll walk you through inspection/wear items early so
   there are no surprise charges.
2. **Buy it out** — your payoff number vs. what it's actually worth (sometimes
   there's real equity in that gap).
3. **Roll into a new one** — with current programs, often at a similar payment.

Want to grab 30 minutes for a lease-end review? Early week or weekend better?

Nick
