# Phone Scripts

Frameworks, not word-for-word reads — say them like a human. Goal of nearly
every call: a firm appointment (specific time, both parties confirm) or a
scheduled next call. Voicemails: 20 seconds max, always paired with a text.

---

## Phone-up (inbound sales call)

**Open:** "Great choice calling about that one — it's been getting attention.
I'm Nick, and you are? … {{name}}, best number in case we get cut off?"

**Answer-then-advance pattern (use on every question):**
- *"Is it available?"* → "It's here — I'm looking at it on the board now. When
  were you hoping to see it, today or tomorrow?"
- *"What's your best price?"* → "Fair question. Honest answer: the real number
  depends on the vehicle in front of us and whether there's a trade — anything
  I quote blind would be a guess, and I don't guess with your money. What I
  WILL do is have the exact figures, trade number included, printed and
  waiting when you come in. Today at 4:30 or tomorrow at 10?"
- *"I'm just calling around."* → "Smart way to do it. Let me make it worth the
  call: what are the two or three things that matter most — price, payment, or
  the right equipment? … Based on that, here's what I'd actually look at…"

**Appointment close (alternative choice + anchor):** "I've got {{time A}} or
{{time B}} — which is easier? … Done. I'll pull it up front with a
sold-pending tag on it, and I'll text you a confirmation right now so you have
my name and number."

## Outbound: internet lead, Day 0 (call twice, back-to-back)

**Live:** "Hi {{name}}, Nick at Berger Chevrolet — you just asked about the
{{model}} online, so I wanted to reach you while it's fresh. I'm standing 50
feet from it. What made that one catch your eye? … {{listen}} … Want to come
see it while it's still here — today or tomorrow?"

**Voicemail:** "Hi {{name}}, Nick at Berger Chevrolet about the {{model}} you
just asked about — good news on it. I'm texting you a video of the actual
vehicle right now. My direct line is {{phone}}."

## Orphan owner intro call

"Hi {{name}}, Nick at Berger Chevrolet. Not a sales call — {{previous
salesperson}} moved on, and I've been asked to personally look after their
customers, which includes you and your {{model}}. I just wanted to introduce
myself and ask: how's it treating you? … Anything I can chase down for you on
the service side? … Last thing — save my number: anything Berger-related, I'm
your inside guy now."

## Lease renewal call

"Hi {{name}}, Nick at Berger. Your {{model}} lease matures around
{{month}}, and I hate when people get rushed into a decision at the deadline —
so I'm calling early. You've got three options: turn it in, buy it out, or
roll into a new one, and the right answer depends on your numbers. Can we grab
30 minutes this week to lay all three out? I'll have your payoff, your car's
market value, and the new-lease programs ready. {{Time A}} or {{time B}}?"

## Equity call

"{{name}}, Nick at Berger Chevrolet — quick one. Clean {{model}}s like yours
are bringing unusually strong money right now, and when that happens I call my
owners. Two questions: roughly what's the mileage? … And if I ran your exact
number and it put you in a new one around your current payment — worth
seeing? … I'll run it today and call you back with the real figure either way."

## Service-drive approach (in person, waiting lounge — soft)

"Mind if I interrupt your coffee for 30 seconds? I'm Nick from the sales side.
I saw the estimate on your {{model}} — before you sink {{amount}} into it,
would it be useful to see two numbers? What it's worth as a trade *as-is*
(the repair becomes our problem), and what that repair money looks like as a
down payment. Ten minutes while you're already waiting, and if keeping it is
the smarter math, I'll tell you straight."

## Be-back call

"{{name}}, Nick at Berger — you drove the {{model}} on {{day}}. Two things:
first, {{the real new info}}. Second, when you left, the sticking point was
{{the honest objection from the note}} — I've been working on exactly that.
Worth 15 more minutes this week?"

## Day-3 sold check-in (the review + referral call)

"{{name}}! Nick. Three days in — how's the {{model}}? … Any button you haven't
figured out? … Love it. Two quick favors while I've got you: if I text you my
Google review link, would you give me two minutes? Mentioning my name is how
the next family finds me. … And who's the next person in your world who'll
need a vehicle? A name's all I need — I'll take care of them like I took care
of you."

## Voicemail rules (all plays)

- 20 seconds. Name, store, ONE hook, callback number, "texting you now."
- Never leave two voicemails in a row without a text/email between.
- Second same-day call on new leads: don't leave a second voicemail; let it
  ring — many people answer the second attempt.
