# Social Scripts: Video Shot Lists

All videos: phone, vertical, 30–90 seconds, natural light, hook in the first
3 seconds, captions on (most social video plays muted). Done beats perfect —
volume and consistency outrank production value.

---

## 1. Inventory walkaround (3×/week; also sent 1:1 to specific customers)

**Structure (60–90s):**
1. **Hook (0–3s), shot mid-action, not standing still:** "This is the
   cleanest {{year}} {{model}} I've had all month — let me show you why."
2. **Three best features (10–20s each)** — show, don't list: open the tailgate,
   fold the seats, start it and let them hear it. Pick the three that match
   who buys this vehicle.
3. **The honest beat (5s):** "One thing to know: {{real flaw or limitation}}."
   This single line converts more than everything else — it's why they trust
   the rest of the video.
4. **CTA (5s):** "I'm Nick at Berger Chevrolet in Grand Rapids. If this one's
   for you, message me before it isn't here — I'll hold it for a look."

**1:1 variant:** same video but open with their name: "{{name}}, shot this for
you before it goes on the site." Send via DriveCentric/work text.

## 2. Delivery video (every delivery, with permission — 15–30s)

1. New owner next to the vehicle (or getting keys / bow pull).
2. One question to them: "What made you pick it?" or "First road trip's to
   where?" — their one-line answer IS the video.
3. Your close: "Another one home. Congrats {{first name}} — enjoy it!"

Post to FB/IG (tag if they're comfortable) + text them the clip — they'll
share it to *their* network, which is the actual play. Log to Content
Calendar.

## 3. Answer-a-question (2×/week — infinite topic supply)

Take a real question a customer asked this week. To camera, 45–60s:

1. **Hook = the question itself:** "'Should I buy out my lease or turn it
   in?' — I get this every week. Real answer in 45 seconds."
2. **The honest answer** — including the part that doesn't favor the
   dealership. That's the whole brand.
3. **CTA:** "More questions like this — my inbox is open. Nick, Berger
   Chevrolet, Grand Rapids."

**Topic bank:** lease vs. buy • what's my trade actually worth • how dealers
set used prices • rate shopping honestly • EV tax credit basics • AWD vs. 4WD
in Michigan winter • when to buy (month-end truth) • what CSI surveys actually
do • first-time buyer credit basics • why the 'internet price' has an asterisk
(and Berger's doesn't).

## 4. Quick-hit reels (fill days — 15–30s, low effort)

- "Just landed" — one pass around a fresh trade-in, one line, "won't make it
  to the weekend."
- Before/after detail shots.
- "Three trucks under ${{price}} on our lot right now" — three 5-second clips.
- Store life: the 6 AM lot walk, snow-covered inventory in January, the team.

## Recording hygiene

- Wipe the lens. Every time. This is 80% of "production quality."
- Talk 10% louder and 20% slower than feels natural; smile before hitting
  record.
- Golden hour (morning/late afternoon) flatters paint; avoid harsh noon glare.
- Batch: shoot the week's 3 walkarounds in one 45-minute block (`/social`
  builds the batch list).
