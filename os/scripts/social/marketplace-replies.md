# Social Scripts: Marketplace Reply Playbook

The game: reply in under 5 minutes, be the first helpful HUMAN (everyone else
sends "Is this available?" auto-replies), move to phone/text, then to an
appointment. Never negotiate price in Messenger.

---

## "Is this available?" (the 80% opener)

"Yes — it's here and I'm looking at it now. I'm Nick (I sell at Berger
Chevrolet in Grand Rapids, so everything's inspected and financing/trades are
easy). Want me to shoot you a quick video walkaround of it right now?"

> The instant video offer is the separator. Nobody else offers it. If they say
> yes, shoot a 45–60s video ON that unit and send it within the hour.

## "What's your lowest price?"

"Fair question. It's listed at ${{price}} and priced against the market, so
there's not a big cushion built in — but the real answer depends on whether
there's a trade and how you're paying. Easiest path: come see it, and if you
love it we'll land on numbers in person. I promise nobody plays games — it's a
dealership deal with a CarFax, not a parking-lot handshake. When are you free,
{{day A}} or {{day B}}?"

## "Any problems with it? / Why so cheap?"

"Honest answer: {{the flaw from the listing + anything else true}}. Beyond
that it passed our shop's inspection. I put the flaws in the listing on
purpose — I'd rather you trust me than be surprised. Want the CarFax? I'll
send it right now."

## "Will you take $X?" (lowball)

"I appreciate a direct offer. That number won't get it done — it's priced off
live market data and it's been getting real interest. Here's what I can do:
come drive it, and if it's the right vehicle we'll sharpen the pencil face to
face, plus your trade might close the gap more than you'd think. {{day A}} or
{{day B}}?"

## "Is the price negotiable if I pay cash?"

"Cash and financed deals land in the same place on price these days — the
market sets it, not the payment method. But bring your numbers and your trade
and we'll make it work as hard as it can. When works?"

## "Can you deliver / I'm far away"

"Depending on distance, {{honest store policy}}. What I always do: a full
personal video tour first — every flaw, every angle, cold start — so the trip
is never wasted. Want that video today?"

## "I have a trade — what'll you give me?"

"Love it — trades make these deals work. I can't give a real number without
eyes on it (anyone who does is guessing high to hook you, then cutting it at
the desk). Send me: year/model/miles + 3 photos, and I'll get you an honest
RANGE today, exact number when you're here. Deal?"

## Moving to phone (after 3–4 messages, always)

"Messenger's slow for the good stuff — what's your best number? I'll text you
the video + CarFax there, and you'll have my direct line. I'm Nick:
{{phone}}."

## Booked → confirm like a real appointment

Run the full confirmation sequence from

## Ghosted after interest

Day 2: "Still interested in the {{model}}? It's had more messages — happy to
tell you honestly where it stands." Day 5: "It's {{still here / getting close
to sold}} — last touch from me either way. Want it held for a look this week?"
