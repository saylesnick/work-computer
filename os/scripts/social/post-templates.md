# Social Scripts: Post Templates (the weekly content mix)

The 5-3-2-adapted weekly mix from
`../../playbooks/lead-engines/social-video.md` §3: 3× value/inventory,
2× education, 1× social proof, 1× human. `/social` generates a week from these
templates. Voice: helpful neighbor who happens to sell Chevys — never
dealership-ad voice.

---

## Value / inventory posts (3×/week)

**Just landed:**
> Fresh trade just hit the lot: {{year}} {{model}} {{trim}}, {{mileage}} miles,
> {{one-line condition story}}. These don't usually survive the weekend.
> Want first look before it's on the site? Message me. 📸 {{3–5 photos or
> walkaround video}}

**Deal spotlight (real desk numbers only):**
> What does ${{payment}}/mo actually get you right now? This {{year}}
> {{model}} with {{key equipment}}. {{one honest qualifier: "with approved
> credit, ${{down}} down"}}. If your current payment buys less car than this,
> let's talk.

**Inventory roundup:**
> Three {{category}}s on our lot under ${{price}} right now:
> 1. {{unit 1 — one line}}
> 2. {{unit 2 — one line}}
> 3. {{unit 3 — one line}}
> Which one's the sleeper? (I know which one I'd take.) DM me for photos.

## Education posts (2×/week — the shareable trust-builders)

**Myth-buster:**
> Car-buying myth #{{n}}: "{{myth, e.g., paying cash gets you a better
> price}}." Truth: {{honest 2–3 sentence answer, including the part that
> doesn't favor dealers}}. Save this for your next purchase — or ask me the
> question you've always wondered about. Honest answers only. 👇

**Michigan-specific:**
> {{seasonal/local topic: "AWD vs 4WD for West Michigan winters", "what road
> salt actually does to your resale value", "winter tire math"}}.
> {{3–4 sentence genuinely useful answer}}.

**Q&A repost:** take this week's answer-a-question video
(`video-shot-lists.md` §3) and post with:
> A customer asked me this on Tuesday and I realized most people don't know
> the answer: {{question}}. {{video}}

## Social proof (1×/week)

**Delivery post:**
> {{first name(s)}} came in for "{{what they asked for}}" and left with this
> {{model}}. {{one human detail from the deal — kid's reaction, the road-trip
> plan, third {{model}} they've bought}}. Thank you for trusting me with it.
> Who's next? {{delivery photo/video, with permission}}

**Review screenshot:**
> This is why I do this job. {{screenshot of Google review naming Nick}}
> Thank you, {{first name}}. (Want the same experience? You know where I am.)

**Milestone:**
> Family #{{n}} this month drove home today. Grateful for every single one —
> especially the ones who came from a friend's recommendation. That trust is
> everything.

## Human posts (1×/week)

> {{6 AM lot walk photo}} Nobody sees this part. First one here, coffee #1,
> walking every row so I know exactly what's here when you ask.

> {{Grand Rapids life: Bridge Street tacos, ArtPrize, first snow on the lot,
> Whitecaps game}} — {{one line, zero sales content}}.

> Unpopular opinion from a car guy: {{lightly spicy honest take, e.g., "you
> probably don't need the biggest engine — you need the right tires"}}.
> Fight me in the comments.

## Local group responses (when someone asks for vehicle help)

> {{Genuinely helpful public answer to their actual question — 3–4 sentences,
> no pitch, mention non-Chevy options if honest}}. I sell at Berger Chevrolet
> so I see this stuff daily — happy to DM you a couple specific options if
> useful, zero pressure either way.

## Rules

- Real numbers only from real desk quotes; include required disclaimers per
  store policy.
- Customer photos/tags: explicit permission, every time.
- Reply to every comment within the hour when possible — the algorithm and
  the humans both reward it.
- Log posts to Content Calendar (`/log`); note weekly which post earned the
  most reach/DMs — `/social` uses that to steer next week's mix.
