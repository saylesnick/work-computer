Field Manual

One sheet on how to run the OS: the daily loop, what every dashboard panel means, and the exact commands that drive it. DriveCentric stays your CRM — this system exists to make customers and sharpen every stage of the sale.

The Loop

This is the whole job. Everything else is detail.
  1. Morning · 2 minType /daily to Claude. You get today's plan: content to post, the mining play, referral touches due, your appointments, and pace vs. goal.
  2. During the day · ~60–90 min of self-generated workWork the plan. 1 post + 1 video, 10 mining outreaches, referral touches — plus your normal DriveCentric workplan. Tick items off on the dashboard's Today panel as you go.
  3. Whenever a lead or moment hitsUse the situational commands. New lead → /new-lead. Not sure what to send someone → /follow-up. Customer pushes back → /objection. Content needed → /social.
  4. End of shift · 1 sentenceType /log with your numbers. "/log 22 calls, 8 conversations, 2 appts set, 1 shown. Sold Jones the used Silverado, $1,800 front — Marketplace lead." That single sentence updates Airtable, and the dashboard moves.
  5. Weekly · 30 minType /review. Real funnel ratios, which engine is producing, one focus for next week. Then batch next week's content with /social.

Rules of the Road

Built into everything Claude drafts for you
Nothing sends itself.
Every email, text, and script is a draft you copy into DriveCentric, work email, or the dealer texting tool. Texts go only to opted-in customers through dealership tools.
No invented numbers.
Prices, payments, rates, and trade values show as {{placeholders}} until you fill them from the desk. Honesty is the brand.
Every sold deal gets a source.
When you /log a sale, name where it came from (referral, Marketplace, orphan, be-back…). That one habit is what makes the Engines panel — and every double-down decision — real.
DriveCentric is still the CRM.
Tasks, workplan, and customer records live there. The OS layers on top; it never replaces store process.

Quick Fixes

The four questions you'll actually have
Dashboard says "Snapshot" instead of "Live."
Open it inside claude.ai and allow the Airtable permission when asked. If it says reconnect: claude.ai Settings → Connectors → Airtable.
I want to change my monthly goals.
Tell Claude ("set my September goal to 18 units") or edit the month's row in the Airtable base "Sales Command Center" → Goals & Scoreboard. The dashboard follows within ~2 minutes.
I logged something wrong.
Just tell Claude — "that Jones deal was a be-back, not a referral" — and it corrects Airtable. /review also cross-checks totals weekly and fixes drift.
My checklist reset / looks different on my phone.
By design: the Today checklist is per-day and per-device. The numbers that matter live in Airtable via /log, never in the checkboxes.
